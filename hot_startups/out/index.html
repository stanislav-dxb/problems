Gaps we noticed

Suggestions, not facts
  • Jack & Jill put an AI agent on both sides of hiring and Zero replaced the sales CRM. Nobody on the list has built the same two-sided agent for the other big matching markets: finding a doctor, a tradesperson or a tenant.
  • AQEMIA and EveryCarbon pair physics and biology with AI to design medicines and plastics. The same recipe is missing for food and fertiliser, where farmers need new molecules just as badly.
  • Stream and Thatch reach millions of hourly workers by selling through the employer. No one uses that channel yet for what those workers struggle with next: rent deposits, childcare or legal help.
  • Kepler Aerospace and Maven Robotics build hardware for governments and warehouses. No entry brings cheap robots or satellite data to smallholder farms in Asia or Africa, where BuuPass shows the phones are already in everyone's pocket.
  • Tarabut and Insurely built consented financial-data pipes for the Gulf and the Nordics. Latin America and Southeast Asia, where Ontop and Bumame operate, still lack that layer, and whoever builds it will own the region's fintech back end.
