Gaps we noticed

Suggestions, not facts
  • AI medical scribes like Nabla, Tandem Health and Voize all target doctors and nurses; nobody here builds the same speak-instead-of-type note automation for vets, dentists or mental health therapists, professions with the same paperwork burden and much less competition.
  • Space hardware builders like The Exploration Company, MBRYONICS, Kepler Aerospace and Open Cosmos are multiplying, but nobody insures the satellites and missions they launch; a Stoïk-style insurer built specifically for space assets and mission failure could pair naturally with this wave.
  • Cross-border fintechs like barq, Aspora, Ontop and Stream serve migrants, expats and gig workers, but none of them serve refugees or asylum seekers, who have similar cross-border money needs plus no credit history and often no bank account at all.
  • AI-for-science startups like AQEMIA, Cradle and Cubase Bio apply generative AI and physics to drugs and proteins; the same approach could be pointed at breeding new crop varieties faster, a natural extension for a company like xFarm that already has farm-level data but no molecular design layer.
  • AI legal tech is crowded with tools for lawyers and companies (Lawhive, Lexroom, Solve Intelligence, Summize, Magnar, netLex), but almost all of it serves English or Latin American markets; a similar AI legal assistant built for individuals navigating everyday legal problems in Middle Eastern or Southeast Asian legal systems is still missing.
